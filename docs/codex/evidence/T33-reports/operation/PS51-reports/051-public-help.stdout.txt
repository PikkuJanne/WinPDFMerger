    PS C:\>.\WinPDFMerge.ps1 'C:\Work\Papers\ToMerge'

    -------------------------- EXAMPLE 2 --------------------------
    
    PS C:\>.\WinPDFMerge.ps1 'C:\Work\Papers\ToMerge' -OutputFolder 'C:\Work\Merged'
    
    Uses the existing separate output directory.
    
    
    
    
    -------------------------- EXAMPLE 3 --------------------------
    
    PS C:\>.\WinPDFMerge.ps1 'C:\Work\Papers\ToMerge' -SkipEmail
    
    Creates the master without discovering or launching Ghostscript.
    
    
    
    
    -------------------------- EXAMPLE 4 --------------------------
    
    PS C:\>.\WinPDFMerge.ps1 'C:\Work\Papers\ToMerge' -EmailPreset ebook
    
    Selects the ebook email preset. A candidate without a size benefit is omitted.
